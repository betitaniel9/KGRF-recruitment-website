WHAT IS KGRF?

KGRF is a squadron of War Thunder players based in the Philippines. open to pilots, tankers, and sailors from anywhere in the world.

We fly, we grind, we win, we lose. but mostly, we just enjoy playing with people who feel like friends, not teammates.

If that sounds like your kind of squad, you're already one of us.
