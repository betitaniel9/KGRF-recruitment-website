About Us

Kampfgruppe Filipinas (KGRF) is a War Thunder squadron built on teamwork, discipline, and a passion for military aviation and armored warfare. United by our Filipino roots, we build a community where players improve, work together, and win as one.

Whether you are an experienced pilot, a skilled tanker, or a player eager to learn, KGRF welcomes anyone willing to contribute, improve, and stand with their squadmates.

Requirements

What we ask of every KGRF member before they join the line.

  • Weekly Participation Join squadron battles or practice nights at least once a week.
  • Mic and Discord A working mic and Discord, so we can call targets and coordinate.
